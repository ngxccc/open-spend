# Open Spend

Open Spend is a mobile personal expense and financial tracking application.

## Language

**Expense**:
A financial transaction representing money spent by the user.
_Avoid_: Cost, outgoing, payment

**Income**:
A financial transaction representing money received by the user.
_Avoid_: Revenue, incoming, earning

**Category**:
A classification label assigned to expenses or incomes for grouping and analytics.
_Avoid_: Tag, bucket, label

**Wallet**:
A financial container or account holding balances and tracking transactions.
_Avoid_: Account, bank, vault
